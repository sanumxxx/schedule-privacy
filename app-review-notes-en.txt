Additional context for App Review — «Расписание в вузе» (com.sanum.schedule)

ORGANIZATION / VENDOR NEUTRAL
The app is NOT tied to any specific university, company, or website. Users supply their own iCalendar (.ics) URL or enter classes manually. Any host that serves a compatible .ics file may be used.

SUPPORTED CALENDAR FORMAT (documented for reviewers)
Public documentation: https://sanumxxx.github.io/schedule-privacy/calendar-format.html
Demo calendar (mock groups, fictional data): https://sanumxxx.github.io/schedule-privacy/demo.html
Direct .ics URL: https://sanumxxx.github.io/schedule-privacy/mock-calendar.ics

Expected VEVENT shape:
- SUMMARY: Discipline name + lesson type in parentheses, e.g. "Programming (Lecture)"
- DESCRIPTION: lines "Группа: <name>" and "Подгруппа: <number>" (Group / Subgroup — labels are parsed as plain text)
- LOCATION: room
- DTSTART / DTEND with timezone

Review steps without login:
1. Open app → choose Teacher or Student
2. Paste demo URL above OR use manual entry
3. View schedule; for Teacher open Journal

No accounts, IAP, UGC sharing, AI, or developer backend.
